# UMBRA direction

A graphite room, quiet labels and four real works. The gallery is the hero. There is no giant slogan across the art and no product object on the right of a headline.

The architectural frames have a consistent world space. Alternating paintings hang on left and right planes. Native scroll moves the viewer forward and slightly toward the next wall. Passing through a portal reveals the next work. The floor, diminishing scale, frame thickness, occlusion and perspective establish depth. All movement is a deterministic function of one progress coordinate and reverses with native scroll.

The only primary controls are room numbers and the current work. The full viewer offers a high resolution detail, artist, date, medium, an original curatorial looking note and the verified museum record. It never claims UMBRA owns these works or represents the museum.

Artworks are CC0 Open Access images, checked against `isPublicDomain=true` and a nonempty primary image. Original pixel sizes are in the public asset manifest. The Turner source is 1959px wide and is preserved at that width, not enlarged to 2560px despite the consistent detail filename.

Rendering is event driven DOM composition with no dependency, permanent RAF, full screen blur, live shadow pass or particle system. The integration reviewer must inspect actual portal transitions, mobile art scale and final detail loading.
