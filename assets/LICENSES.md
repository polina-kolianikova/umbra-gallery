# UMBRA public asset credits

All artwork metadata was verified against The Metropolitan Museum of Art Collection API on 20 October 2026. Each selected object had `isPublicDomain: true` and a usable original image URL. Images are distributed under the museum's Creative Commons Zero Open Access policy: https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access.

- Vincent van Gogh, *Wheat Field with Cypresses*, 1889. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, purchase, The Annenberg Foundation Gift, 1993. Object 436535: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/436535. Original 4000 × 3184 pixels.
- Katsushika Hokusai, *Under the Wave off Kanagawa*, c. 1830 to 1832. The Metropolitan Museum of Art. Object 45434: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/45434. Original 3859 × 2594 pixels.
- Paul Cézanne, *Still Life with Apples and a Pot of Primroses*, c. 1890. The Metropolitan Museum of Art. Object 435882: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/435882. Original 3807 × 3021 pixels.
- Joseph Mallord William Turner, *Venice, from the Porch of Madonna della Salute*, c. 1835. The Metropolitan Museum of Art. Object 437853: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/437853. Original 1959 × 1461 pixels.

The full source URLs, medium, credit lines, dimensions and local derivatives are preserved in `artworks.json`. WebP derivatives were resized downward only. High detail versions are loaded on demand. The Turner detail keeps the original width.

Typography: Italiana and Manrope from Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Local official WOFF2 distributions and complete licences are included in `fonts/`. Sources: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/italiana and https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope.

Brand, original curatorial observations, spatial layout, CSS and interactions were authored for this independent concept. UMBRA is not affiliated with The Metropolitan Museum of Art. No copied reference website assets are included.
